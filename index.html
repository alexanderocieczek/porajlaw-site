§ 1Practice

Advice and solutions for complex cases.

The practice covers the legal side of cross-border business: how a group or deal is structured, the agreements it enters into, the transactions and investments it engages in, and how it protects itself. Much of the work is unique cases that span several jurisdictions at once, where there is no template to follow and the solution has to be built.

Organisational structure

Designing and reorganising groups that span jurisdictions.

  • Holding and group structures across jurisdictions
  • Reorganisations, including moving a group under a new parent company
  • Ownership structures for companies and high-value assets
  • Shareholder arrangements, share classes and governance
  • Incentive structures

Contracts and international agreements

Drafting and negotiating agreements between parties in different countries.

  • Developing practical deal structures
  • Shareholder, investment and joint venture agreements
  • Distribution, agency and licensing agreements
  • Framework and partnership agreements
  • Governing law, jurisdiction and dispute resolution

M&A, due diligence and investments

All legal aspects of international M&A and cross-border investment.

  • Term sheets and letters of intent
  • Legal due diligence, buy side and sell side
  • Share and asset purchase agreements
  • Warranties, indemnities, earn-outs and closing
  • Cross-border investments and investor documentation
